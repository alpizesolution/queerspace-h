Anonymous WLW chat on QUEERSPACE PH

QUEERSPACE PH is an anonymous chat space for women who love women (WLW) in the Philippines. You can start a one-to-one conversation in a few taps, without creating an account and without sharing your real name.

How a 1-on-1 chat starts

  1. Pick a pseudonym and an avatar. You get a random name and a small animal avatar, and you can change both. There are no photo profiles.
  2. Tap “Find Your Someone” for a quick match, or choose “Custom match” to say what you are looking for: friendship, dating, casual chat, a deep talk, or open to anything.
  3. Add filters only if you want them. You can pick chat topics, a place in the Philippines (an island group, region or city), and an age range. Your age range is private and is never shown to anyone.
  4. Get paired with someone who is online right now, and start talking. You can reply to a message by swiping it, react to it, or skip to someone new.
  5. The chat ends when either of you leaves, and nothing is kept afterwards.

What stays anonymous

  • You do not need an account. An anonymous ID is created for you automatically in your browser.
  • You are known by a pseudonym and an animal avatar, not a name or a photo.
  • Your birthday is stored privately. Your age is hidden by default and is shown to a chat partner only if you turn on “Show my age”.
  • Details such as gender identity or orientation are optional and stay hidden unless you choose to show them on your profile.

Staying in touch, only if you both want to

If a conversation clicks, you can send a Signal: a gentle, optional note that says you would like to stay in touch. The other person decides privately whether to accept, and nothing else is sent. A Signal can only be sent to someone you are chatting with right now, so it is never a way to contact strangers.

Safety

  • QUEERSPACE PH is for adults, 18 and over. Members confirm their birthday when they join; this is self-reported and is not identity verification.
  • You can skip, leave, block or report someone at any moment.
  • Community guidelines ask everyone to be kind, respectful and careful with their own privacy and other people’s.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your anonymous ID lives in your browser, so clearing site data starts you fresh.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. QUEERSPACE PH works in a mobile browser and can be added to your home screen like an app.
